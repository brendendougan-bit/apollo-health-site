The medicine

How GLP-1 medicines work

Semaglutide and tirzepatide mimic natural gut hormones. They slow how fast the stomach empties and act on appetite signals in the brain. Clinicians usually start at a low dose and raise it in steps. The most common side effects are stomach-related, like nausea, especially while the dose goes up. These medicines also carry serious risks, including a boxed warning about thyroid tumors, so they are not right for everyone.

The difference

Compounded vs brand name

A compounded medicine is made by a state-licensed pharmacy for one patient's prescription. It is not FDA-approved, and the FDA does not review compounded drugs for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they are sold. Now that the semaglutide and tirzepatide shortages are over, pharmacies can't make routine copies of these drugs. A prescriber has to document why a patient needs a compounded version.

The basics

What a peptide actually is

A peptide is a short chain of amino acids. Some are approved medicines. Many are still being studied, and the human evidence varies a lot from one peptide to the next.
