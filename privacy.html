Privacy

How we handle your information

Apollo Health is not yet open. This policy covers the waitlist on this website and takes effect when the waitlist opens. We'll update it, and tell you, before we offer care.

Privacy Policy

Who we are. Apollo Health LLC ("Apollo", "we") runs apollo-online-health.shop. Contact us about privacy at bdougan97@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details you give us: your email address, your state, and, if you choose one, what brings you here (weight care, peptide therapy, NAD+ or glutathione, or just learning).
  • Basic technical data: like any website, the services that deliver this page (our web host, GitHub Pages, and Google Fonts for typefaces) receive your IP address and browser details when your browser asks them for files. We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising pixels on this site.

How we use it

  • To email you when Apollo opens in your state.
  • To plan which states to open in first.

We don't sell your information. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with advertising platforms.

Who receives it

Formspree, a form service, receives your waitlist submission and passes it to our inbox, acting on our instructions as our service provider. Our email provider stores the messages we receive and send. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and no longer than 24 months after you join the waitlist unless you become a patient (patient records are covered by a separate notice we'll publish before we open).

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe with the link in any email from us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing bdougan97@gmail.com. We'll respond within 45 days.
  • Depending on your state, you may have additional rights. We honor them for every waitlist member, wherever they live.

Children

This site and our waitlist are for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to waitlist data to the people who need it to run the waitlist.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Some state laws, including Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut's consumer health data law, treat information that links you to a health interest as consumer health data. This section explains how we handle it.

Consumer health data we collect

If you choose an answer to "What brings you here?", we collect that health interest (weight care, peptide therapy, or NAD+ or glutathione) together with your email address and state. We collect it only from you, through the waitlist form, and only with your consent.

Why we collect and use it

To email you when Apollo opens in your state, and to plan which services and states to launch first. We don't use it for any other purpose without asking you first.

Who we share it with

Only our service providers that handle the waitlist for us: Formspree (form processing) and our email provider. We don't share it with any other third party or affiliate, and we never sell it.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, and get a copy, including a list of anyone we've shared it with.
  • Withdraw your consent, and have your data deleted.

Email bdougan97@gmail.com to use these rights. We'll respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll answer the appeal in writing within 45 days, and if you disagree with the result you can contact your state attorney general.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with its date. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we'll ask for your consent first.
